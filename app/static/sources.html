Full FMV table used by this app

For each category we record three values: low (used when condition is "fair"), median (default; used when condition is "good"), and high (used when condition is "excellent / like new"). These should fall inside the published Salvation Army and Goodwill ranges for the same item category in "good used condition or better" — the standard IRS Publication 526 §A.2 sets for clothing and household items.

Edit any row to override the bundled defaults — overrides are saved to your DB and apply on the next item you save. Customized rows are highlighted; click Reset to restore that category's defaults.

What this tool is not

  • Not a tax return. The app produces a record you attach to your records and use to fill out IRS Form 8283 if your total exceeds $500.
  • Not an appraisal. Single items or "groups of similar items" worth more than $5,000 require a qualified appraisal under Pub. 561 — this app cannot substitute for that.
  • Not legal/tax advice. The values are good-faith estimates based on published guides; the taxpayer is responsible for the final FMV claimed.

LLM endpoint

Any OpenAI-compatible /v1 endpoint with vision support — Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM, llama.cpp, etc. Saved to the database, so it persists across restarts.

Vision model

Pin a specific model on the endpoint above. Leave blank to auto-detect the first vision-capable model on each request. Changes apply immediately.

HTTPS / TLS

Serve the app over HTTPS so phones don't warn about an insecure connection and the camera works without a hitch in iOS Safari. The first time, the app generates a self-signed cert covering localhost + every detected LAN IP. Browsers will warn the first visit — accept it.

Checking…

Use your own certificate

Skip the browser warning by uploading a real cert from Let's Encrypt or mkcert. Both files must be PEM-encoded; the private key must match the certificate's public key.

Or, do it from the shell: drop cert.pem and key.pem directly into the data/certs/ directory and restart the server. Auto-detected on next start.

Factory reset

Wipe every item, drop-off, donor, cash gift, FMV override, setting, and uploaded photo. Daily SQLite backups under data/backups/ are preserved as a safety net.

About
