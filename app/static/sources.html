Full FMV table used by this app

For each category we record three values: low (used when condition is "fair"), median (default; used when condition is "good"), and high (used when condition is "excellent / like new"). These should fall inside the published Salvation Army and Goodwill ranges for the same item category in "good used condition or better" — the standard IRS Publication 526 §A.2 sets for clothing and household items.

What this tool is not

  • Not a tax return. The app produces a record you attach to your records and use to fill out IRS Form 8283 if your total exceeds $500.
  • Not an appraisal. Single items or "groups of similar items" worth more than $5,000 require a qualified appraisal under Pub. 561 — this app cannot substitute for that.
  • Not legal/tax advice. The values are good-faith estimates based on published guides; the taxpayer is responsible for the final FMV claimed.
